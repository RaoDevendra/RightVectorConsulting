Privacy Policy

RightVector Consulting LLC (“RightVector,” “I,” “me”) respects your privacy. This page explains what this website collects and how it is used.

What I collect

The contact form collects only the information you choose to submit: your name, work email, organization, role, the reason for your inquiry, and any message you add. If you book a call through a scheduling tool, that service collects the details you enter there and handles them under its own privacy policy. If you use the form or email link, your message is sent to me by email through your own email provider.

How I use it

I use this information only to reply to your inquiry and to schedule and prepare for a conversation. I do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list without asking.

Confidentiality

What you share stays confidential. I’m glad to sign an NDA before we discuss specifics.

Analytics and cookies

This website may use basic analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If this changes, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, and form submissions and scheduling are handled by third-party services. Those services receive the information needed to provide their function.
