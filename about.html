Bio

Devendra Rao is a technology delivery and transformation executive with 25+ years leading enterprise delivery, modernization, and P&L-accountable operations in complex healthcare environments. He founded RightVector Consulting after leading a healthcare IT operation through a turnaround for a client, following long tenures leading enterprise technology at a national healthcare organization and at a large health system.

He has worked both sides of the table: as an executive on the buyer side who bought and governed technology, and as the senior partner accountable to a client from the vendor side. He is repeatedly brought in to stabilize delivery organizations that have lost their footing, replace firefighting with governance, and rebuild client trust on a compressed timeline. Outside advisory work, he builds native iOS apps, including RecallForge. Based in Atlanta; works with clients nationwide, across regulated industries.

Selected record

  • Raised client satisfaction from 68% to 87% in 90 days and cleared a 1,274-item aged backlog to zero.
  • Scaled a national healthcare web-services platform roughly 2,000 times, from 50,000 to 107 million annual transactions, while holding a one-second response SLA at 99% compliance.
  • Led an assessment of about 350 applications through to an executive readout that secured buy-in for the first wave of a multi-year investment roadmap.
  • Directed technology portfolios of $50M or more a year, and drove $1M in annual run-rate savings by growing platform adoption across autonomous divisions without formal authority over them.
